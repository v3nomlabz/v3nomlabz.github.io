    07 FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Can't find what you need? Talk to a human →

    Which file formats do you accept?

    The instant quote lab supports STL, OBJ and 3MF. For STEP, IGES, Fusion or other CAD formats, send the file via WhatsApp or email and we will quote it manually, usually within a few hours.

    How accurate is the instant price?

    Very close. It's calculated from your model's real volume, surface area and height. Every order gets a quick human review, and if anything changes (for example, a model needs heavy supports), we confirm the final price with you before printing. Nothing is charged until you approve.

    How long does it take?

    Standard orders typically ship in 3–5 working days. Express production ships in 48 hours. Large batches and big cosplay pieces may take longer, and we always tell you upfront.

    What if my model is bigger than the build volume?

    We can split large models into interlocking sections and bond them after printing. The quote lab will flag oversize parts. Send them over and we will plan the split for you.

    I don't have a 3D file. Can you help?

    Absolutely. Our design service turns sketches, photos, drawings or broken parts into print-ready models. Reach out with what you have and we will scope it.

    Which material should I choose?

    PLA for looks and prototypes, PETG or ABS for functional parts, ASA for outdoor use, TPU for flexible parts, Nylon-CF for maximum stiffness, and resin for fine detail. Unsure? Mention your use-case and we will recommend the best fit.
